Heritage & Milestones

Twenty-Five Years, Chapter by Chapter

Each chapter measured not by opening night, but by what still stands — buildings restored, teams grown, and guests who returned for a generation.

1999

The Founding — A Villa on Lake Como

Élodie Marchand and Julian Ashford-Vane acquire a shuttered 19th-century villa and reopen it with twelve rooms, one dining room and a staff of twenty-four. Aurelia Hospitality & Heritage is formally incorporated in Paris that autumn.

Founded Lake Como 12 Keys
2004

The First Landmark Resort

Aurelia opens Villa Serena, its first full-scale resort — forty-eight suites, a spa carved into the hillside, and a kitchen that earned its first guide distinction within eighteen months of service.

Flagship 48 Suites First Award
2011

The $50M Heritage Expansion

A major capital programme acquires and restores two Haussmann-era townhouses in Paris and Marylebone, adding the group’s first private members’ club. Portfolio value crosses fifty million euros for the first time.

$50M Expansion Paris London
2018

Alpine Collection & Fine Dining

Two chalets in Courchevel join the portfolio alongside the opening of Maison d’Or in Paris — the group’s first Michelin-starred dining room and the beginning of its recognised fine dining collection.

Courchevel Michelin Star Private Club
2026

A $200M+ Portfolio, Present Day

Fourteen properties across three countries, seven restaurants, 500,000+ guests hosted and a 98% satisfaction rate. Aurelia remains privately held — still guided by the two founders who opened its first twelve rooms.

$200M+ Portfolio 14 Properties 500K+ Guests
